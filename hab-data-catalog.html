Inclusion is not a scientific claim. An environmental source appears because its registered scope makes it a plausible context layer—not because the Lab has shown it predicts a bloom or toxin. Row totals measure storage volume, not independent observations, and related sources can overlap. Nothing here is a water-safety advisory or a statement of current conditions.

How this inventory was built

The starting point is the Lab’s frozen, adversarially reviewed HAB holdings contract and its separate environmental-support contract. Today’s source registry, fetch receipts, and Lab source manifest supply the current row counts, dates, statuses, and observed column names.

HAB-audited sources remain role-labeled: an observation table, forecast, catalog, or document is not automatically a direct toxin measurement. Environmental sources remain context-only.

How to read “columns” and “ready”

For landed sources, column names are measured schema metadata. For registered-only sources, the page shows planned or required fields and labels that basis explicitly. A name alone does not establish a variable’s scientific meaning or units. “Validation passed” means a landed table passed its registered structural checks. “Approved for modeling” is a stricter and separate Lab decision.

Display names are registry labels and can contain editorial shorthand; they are not scientific findings. The source key printed below each name is the stable identity.

A directory-gap audit found no additional unregistered, positive-row direct HAB source. Unregistered environmental caches remain outside the totals until provenance, overlap, and reuse rights are reconciled.
